# Design brief

Status: APPROVED: Survey sheet

## Subject
A personal portfolio for a full-stack developer aiming at SDE internships and entry-level roles. Strongest work: SentinelLink, a real-time, map-based emergency coordination platform. Side interests: photography, video, UI/UX.

## Audience and job
Recruiters and engineers, mostly on phones, 60-90 seconds. The page must prove: can build, can build well, easy to work with. Projects reachable in under 5 seconds.

## What the design should feel like
Precise, calm, and a little cartographic, because the best project lives on maps and dispatch. Personality comes from craft and one memorable moment, not from decoration.

## Memorable moment
The SentinelLink scroll story (see `.agents/skills/sentinellink-scrollstory`). Everything else is quiet.

## Starting direction to challenge (not mandatory): "Survey sheet"
- Concept: a field survey sheet. Cool chart-paper background, ink text, thin contour lines used as structure and as the hero illustration. Incidents are the only warm colour on the page.
- Light palette: chart paper #EDF1F0, ink #12201F, contour #B9C6C2, water blue #2A5C8A, incident #D6246E.
- Dark palette: deep slate #0F1719, text #E6EEEB, contour #26363B, blue #7FA6CC, incident #FF6B9C.
- Type candidates (verify on Google Fonts, self-host): Newsreader + Public Sans, Literata + Archivo, or Young Serif + Work Sans. Pick by trying them with real copy, not by reputation.
- Layout: left-aligned, generous measure, projects as a legend/index rather than a card grid.
- Beware: do not let it drift into a cream-and-serif or neon-dark template.

## Seek out (for inspiration, never to copy)
Print atlases and survey maps, transit signage, field notebooks, editorial layouts from independent publications, and developer portfolios that show process rather than logos.

## Avoid
Everything in `.agents/rules/design-no-slop.md`. Also avoid the look of my old portfolio and the busy GitHub profile README (stat widgets, trophies, GIF banners).
